About Our Cuisine

At Elevate, we craft each dish with intention and care. Our chef sources seasonal ingredients from local purveyors, transforming them into culinary expressions that honor tradition while embracing innovation. Every plate tells a story of craftsmanship, passion, and dedication to excellence.

Signature Collection

Our tasting menu features seven carefully curated courses, each paired with selections from our award-winning wine list. Expect refined presentations, unexpected flavor combinations, and service that anticipates your every need.

  • Pan-seared foie gras with cherry gastrique
  • Wild mushroom risotto with truffle oil
  • Heritage poultry with seasonal vegetable accompaniment
  • Artisanal cheese course with house-made condiments
  • Chocolate tart with caramelized banana purée
